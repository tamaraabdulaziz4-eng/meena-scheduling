⚡ Auto-Generate Schedule

🤖 The solver will generate a schedule based on your current inputs for this branch and month — including staff count, leaves taken, and the settings below. Only M and N shifts are auto-assigned; other shifts can be added manually after.

 · Coverage per section/day follows your Section Month Settings (Min/Max M and Min/Max N per day)
 · Max consecutive working days is enforced per section (default 4). After a full block, the next 2 days are Off (unless AL)
 · No isolated Off days (O) if enabled: Off must be at least 2 consecutive days (configured via Section Month Settings)
 · Half‑month rule: each staff works only one shift type in each half (M-only or N-only), then switches in the second half (with required rest days around the mid‑month switch)
 · Cross‑month rule: the previous month’s ending shifts are considered so you don’t start a new month in an unsafe pattern (e.g. 5 straight work days)
 · Staff work within their Staff Month Settings (Min/Max shifts + Max consecutive) for this month
 · Annual Leave (AL) days are locked in and cannot be scheduled as work
 · Generation runs per section: if one section is infeasible, other sections may still generate (you’ll see diagnostics)
⚠️ If the solver cannot find a valid schedule, your constraints are too tight. To resolve:
1. Adjust Staff Month Settings or Section Month Settings via
2. Reduce the number of leaves (AL) for this month
3. Add more staff to the section (common cause: not enough staff to cover daily Min M+N under 4‑on/2‑off)
