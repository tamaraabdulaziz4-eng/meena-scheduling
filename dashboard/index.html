⚡ Auto-Generate Schedule

🤖 The solver will generate a schedule based on your current inputs for this branch and month — including staff count, leaves taken, and the settings below. Only M and N shifts are auto-assigned; other shifts can be added manually after.

 · At least 1 and at most 2 Night (N) shifts per section per day
 · At least 1 and at most 2 Morning (M) shifts per section per day
 · No Night → Morning shift back-to-back
 · Each staff member works at most their configured max consecutive days in a row
 · Each staff member works within their configured min / max shifts for the month
 · Balanced M and N distribution across staff
 · Annual leave days locked in
 · Min / Max shifts and Max consecutive days are set per staff, per branch, per month — changes apply to this month only and do not carry over to other months
⚠️ If the solver cannot find a valid schedule, your constraints are too tight. To resolve:
1. Click — resets all staff to min 0 · max 17 · max 4 consecutive days
2. Or reduce the number of leaves for this month
3. If it still fails, please contact your system administrator.
